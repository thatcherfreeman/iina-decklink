Device

Set this to what your monitor expects. The picture is scaled to fit it, and only the frame rate is negotiated against the source — so a 1080p film on a UHD monitor is upscaled rather than switching the monitor's mode. The list is what this device actually reports.

Signal

1:1 sends the source at its native size with no resampling, cropping or padding to fit the canvas.
The source track is resampled to 48 kHz and mixed to this many channels. IINA keeps playing its own audio regardless; this is a separate feed for the monitor. Muted while paused, and while playing at anything other than 1×.

Still capture

—
Plugin → Grab Still (or press I) saves the frame currently on the DeckLink output as a 16-bit TIFF, named from the title and timestamp. Playback pauses for the moment it takes to write it.
